You are responsible for your own safety and the safety of others when gardening. Always research plant toxicity before consuming any plant, wear appropriate protective equipment, and follow local laws and regulations. You may not republish, reproduce, or redistribute full articles without prior written permission. For permissions, contact hello@ashwoodbotanicaltales.com.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Our Approach

Ashwood Botanical Tales is an independent, ad-free publication. We believe privacy is a right, not a feature. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the Site and communicate with our readers.

2.2 What We Collect

  • Email address: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address solely to send you gardening updates and seasonal reminders. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.
  • Non-identifying analytics: We use a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies, does not fingerprint users, and does not collect personal data. It only tells us which pages are popular and roughly where readers come from (e.g., search engine, direct visit).
  • Contact information: If you email us directly, we retain that correspondence to respond to your query.

2.3 What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.
  • We do not profile you or build marketing audiences.
  • We do not collect payment information (our Site is free to read).

2.4 Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your email address is your consent (which you provide when you subscribe). You have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal basis for processing non-identifying analytics is our legitimate interest in understanding which content is useful to readers.

2.5 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (typically just your email address).
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for newsletter communications.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@ashwoodbotanicaltales.com. We will respond within 30 days.

2.6 Data Retention

Newsletter email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. If you unsubscribe, we delete your email from our mailing list within 7 days. Direct correspondence is retained for up to 2 years for record-keeping.

2.7 Data Security

We use reputable newsletter and hosting providers who implement industry-standard security measures. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

2.8 Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

3. Cookie Policy

3.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

3.2 Cookies We Use

Ashwood Botanical Tales uses no advertising cookies and no tracking cookies. We may use a single, functional cookie to remember your newsletter subscription preference (so you don't see the sign-up prompt repeatedly). This cookie contains no personal data and expires after 90 days.

3.3 Analytics

We use a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not set cookies. It collects anonymised data such as page views, referral sources, and approximate country. This helps us understand which articles are useful. No personal data is collected, and you cannot be identified from this data.

3.4 Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. This will not affect your ability to read our articles. To learn more about cookies and how to manage them, visit aboutcookies.org.

4. Newsletter

Our newsletter is free and sent approximately once a month. We use a third-party email service provider to manage subscriptions. That provider is bound by data processing agreements that comply with GDPR. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
