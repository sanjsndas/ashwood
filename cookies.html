Cookies Policy

Last updated: 1 March 2026 · Version 2.4

Cookies are small text files that help our little gardening journal feel more like a well-kept garden—remembering your preferences, keeping the gate secure, and letting us know which paths visitors enjoy most. This policy explains how we use them, and how you can manage them.

🍪 The short version: We use a few essential cookies to make the site work, and a couple of optional ones to understand what readers enjoy. No advertising, no creepy tracking. You can turn them off anytime.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide anonymous information to site owners.

Cookies set by us are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by others (like analytics providers) are "third-party cookies." We keep third-party cookies to an absolute minimum.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for a few straightforward purposes, always with your privacy in mind:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function—for example, remembering your cookie consent choice or keeping the site secure.
  • Preference cookies: We may store small preferences, such as your preferred reading layout or whether you’ve dismissed a notice.
  • Analytics cookies: We use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand which articles are read most, how visitors navigate, and where we can improve. The data is aggregated and anonymised.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or sell any cookie data to third parties.

3. The cookies we set

Below is a list of the specific cookies we use and what they do:

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
ashwood_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask on every page. 1 year
ashwood_session Essential Maintains your session while you browse (e.g., if you submit a contact form). Session
ashwood_prefs Preference Remembers small display preferences, like font size or contrast settings. 6 months
_ga_* (or similar) Analytics Anonymised analytics to understand page views and reading patterns. Up to 14 months

We also use a strictly necessary cookie to remember your cookie banner choice. No other third-party cookies are set by default.

4. Third-party cookies

We may use a privacy-focused analytics service to help us understand how the journal is used. This service may set a cookie on your device. We have configured it to anonymise IP addresses and not share data with other platforms. We do not use any social media pixels, advertising cookies, or cross-site trackers.

If we ever embed content from other sites (e.g., a YouTube video about pruning roses), those sites may set their own cookies. We will always try to use privacy-enhanced modes where available. We recommend checking the privacy policies of those third-party services if you have concerns.

5. Your choices & controlling cookies

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies from being set.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note: if you disable essential cookies, some parts of the website may not work properly (e.g., the contact form or preference memory).

You can also set your cookie preferences on our site via the consent banner (if shown) or by adjusting your browser settings.

6. Your consent

When you first visit Ashwood Botanical Tales, you will see a cookie banner asking for your consent to set non-essential cookies. Essential cookies are set automatically to make the site function, as they are necessary for basic operation.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing cookies in your browser or by revisiting the consent banner (usually accessible via a small link in the footer). We do not use pre-ticked boxes or implied consent.

7. How to withdraw consent

If you have previously accepted our use of non-essential cookies and wish to withdraw consent, you can:

  • Delete the cookies from your browser (see your browser’s help pages).
  • Use the cookie preference centre link in the footer (if available).
  • Adjust your browser settings to block cookies from our site.

Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

8. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to check back periodically.

🌿 A final note from the potting shed: We're gardeners, not data collectors. Cookies help us keep the site running smoothly and understand which gardening tales you enjoy most. If you have any questions about how we use cookies, please get in touch—we're happy to explain.
